Cyber Threats Have No Borders.

One email, one call or one request identified in one jurisdiction may use the same approach to reach people elsewhere across APAC.

Cyber threats are becoming more convincing, more personal and more connected.

Across APAC, public reporting points to scams, phishing, impersonation, ransomware and the exploitation of vulnerabilities. Case volumes are not rising uniformly in every jurisdiction. However, selected attack types, losses and AI-enabled impersonation show that the nature of the threat is changing. These trends are not unique to any single country, industry or organization, and remind us that no organization can assume it is immune.

Important data note: The figures on this page are not SMBC incident data. Definitions, populations, periods and reporting methods differ, so the figures must not be used for direct country-to-country comparison.

Satoru SuzukiHead of Information Security, SMBC Asia Pacific

Why now?

Look beyond headline totals to growth rates, losses and changing attack methods.

Because the threat environment is changing. Attackers are increasingly using AI to produce convincing messages, voices and identities at scale, making malicious activity harder to distinguish from legitimate business communication. The result is not only more attacks, but more persuasive attacks.

So what?

Attacks often arrive through ordinary work.

An email. A call. An invoice. A supplier change. A system request.

They reach us personallyPhishing, SMS, QR codes and calls

Phishing, SMS, QR codes and calls reach work and home.

They exploit trustColleagues, suppliers, authorities, IT support

Attackers pose as colleagues, suppliers, authorities or IT support.

They exploit processPayments, approvals, account changes, access requests

Payments, approvals, account changes and access requests can be abused.

They exploit technologyVulnerabilities, gaps and limited visibility

Vulnerabilities, configuration gaps and limited visibility create opportunity.

From local action to regional protection

Action taken in each location helps protect the company across borders.

Local awareness→Early escalation→Regional visibility→Company-wide protection
